The Problem

The Hidden Perils of Municipal Dry Waste

Millions of informal waste workers handle unsorted waste with zero visibility or protection.

💉Puncture & Chemical Hazards

Uncapped syringes, shattered glass bottles, toxic acids, and mercury CFL tubes cause chronic lacerations and infections.

🔥Battery & Explosion Risks

Damaged lithium batteries and aerosol canisters burst during truck transport and compaction, sparking landfill fires.

🗣️Literacy & Language Divide

Informal workers communicate in regional vernacular languages (Tamil, Hindi) and cannot parse complex written manuals.

📦The "Blind Transfer" Gap

Sacks travel without custody records; facility sorters have no advance warning of sack contents before handling.

Our Solution

SafeSack 360 Architecture

Edge computer vision + cryptographic QR chain-of-custody + vernacular voice safety guidance.

1️⃣

No-Touch Gate

Mandatory digital safety checklist confirms gloves, tongs, and eye protection before opening bags.

2️⃣

In-Browser Edge AI

TensorFlow.js runs 100% locally in browser. Zero cloud AI costs, instant inference, and offline capability.

3️⃣

Cryptographic QR

Printable hazard tags encode unique sack ID, hazard classification, risk color code, and zone origin.

4️⃣

Vernacular Audio

Autoplays native spoken alerts in Tamil, Hindi, or English for illiterate or fast-paced facility sorters.

Prototype Demo • Collector Portal

Zero-Touch Intake & Conveyor AI

Role: collector (EMP101)
Feature 1

Mandatory No-Touch PPE Check

Workers must confirm 5 safety requirements (no bare hands, heavy gauntlets, mask, cutter/tongs ready) before the conveyor station unlocks.

Feature 2

In-Browser TensorFlow.js Hazard Scanning

Captures frames every 3 seconds, flashing and animating a CSS conveyor belt. Identifies batteries, sharps, glass, CFLs, and chemical bottles locally.

Feature 3

Automated Risk Scoring & QR Tag Generation

Sack risk escalates to highest detected severity (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW). Generates printable labels with unique QR custody tokens.

Prototype Demo • Disposer Portal

Voice Warnings & Certified Containment

Role: disposer (EMP201)
Feature 1

Autonomous Multilingual Voice Alert

Immediately speaks the highest hazard alert in Tamil, Hindi, or English (e.g. "எச்சரிக்கை. இந்த பையில் பேட்டரிகள் உள்ளன..."). Sorters don't need to read.

Feature 2

Actionable Tool & Container Instructions

Provides localized pickup protocol (e.g. "Use long mechanical tongs, never bare hands") and color-coded receptacle guidance.

Feature 3

Enforced Segregation & QR Tag Retirement

Disposal action is locked until every hazardous object is checked off. Disposer permanently retires or disables the QR tag with audit trail.
